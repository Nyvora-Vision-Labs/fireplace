Prepared for this app with low-cut filtering, stationary spectral noise
gating to remove the recording's steady hiss, a 3,600 Hz high-cut filter,
level adjustment, and a 600 ms equal-power loop crossfade. Saved as
22,050 Hz stereo, 16-bit PCM WAV for gapless playback without compression
artifacts. Log crackles and settling effects use this same cleaned recording.
