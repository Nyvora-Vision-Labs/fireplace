How the fire works

  1. Every log burns for 15 minutes. Drag one from the woodpile onto the fire to start focusing.
  2. Keep feeding it. Each log you add puts 15 more minutes on the clock, up to 8 logs (two hours).
  3. Watch it burn down. At every 15-minute mark a log collapses into the coals and the fire burns a little lower.
  4. Rest when it goes out. When the last log is gone, take a 5-minute break by the embers, then light it again.
